# Client Brief: GreenScape Landscaping

## Business

GreenScape Landscaping — residential and commercial landscaping in Phoenix, AZ. 15 years in business, 12 employees, family-owned.

## Website Goal

Replace the current single-page WordPress site with a multi-page site that ranks for local search terms and converts visitors into quote requests. Current site gets ~200 visits/month, no clear CTAs, no service breakdown.

## Pages Needed

- **Home** — hero with primary CTA (request a quote), overview of services, trust signals (years in business, number of projects, licensed/insured), recent project photos
- **Services** — three service tiers: residential lawn care, commercial maintenance contracts, landscape design and installation. Each needs its own section with pricing guidance (not exact prices — "starting from" ranges)
- **About** — company story (started by founder in his garage, grew to 12-person crew), team photo section, certifications (Arizona ROC licensed, irrigation certified)
- **Gallery** — project showcase grid, filterable by residential/commercial
- **Contact** — quote request form (name, email, phone, service type, property size), phone number, service area map (Phoenix metro, Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler)

## Tone

Professional but approachable. Not corporate — they're a local family business that takes pride in their work. Avoid jargon. The founder should feel comfortable reading this copy aloud to a customer.

## Keywords

- Phoenix landscaping
- residential landscaping Phoenix AZ
- commercial landscape maintenance Phoenix
- landscape design Scottsdale
- lawn care Tempe Mesa Chandler

## Existing Site

The current WordPress site has these published pages:
- Home (outdated, single-page layout with everything crammed in)
- Contact (basic form, works but ugly)

The Services, About, and Gallery pages need to be created new.

## Competitor Notes

Main local competitors: Desert Verde Landscaping, Mountainscapes AZ, EcoLawn Phoenix. GreenScape differentiates on reliability (same crew on every job) and their 15-year track record.
